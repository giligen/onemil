## 3. The two levers

Income = capital × per-unit edge. Only two things move it:

1. **Evidence** (the next 10 weeks): each book proving live within its band
   turns the haircut from ×0.5–0.7 into ×1.0. That alone is +40–60% income
   at the same size, and it is the only lever that costs nothing.
2. **Capital**: the drawdown rows above are the constraint, not the P&L rows.
   A retirement book must survive its worst quarter without a decision made
   in fear. Rule I recommend: **portfolio worst-case drawdown ≤ 25% of
   dedicated capital.** Row 2 needs ~$150K dedicated; row 3 ~$210K; row 4
   ~$340K.

## 4. The plan (three phases, gates already agreed on 8/14, refined with 9/5 numbers)

**Phase A — validate (now → 11/15/26). Size for evidence, not income.**
- ORB: 9/12 gate $10K→$25K (both weeks green + parity-clean, validation P&L
  > 0, no unexplained violations). $25K→$50K on the same rule 4 weeks later.
- Bull flag: unified trail live 9/8; ladder $60→$250→$1K→$2K gated on the
  parity AGREE streak (4 of 10); promote regen-7 as the production cache.
- Ignition: $50 through 10/15; verdict on fills (≤ 10 bps vs model) and the
  exit gap; then $250 if passed, staged-only if the chase cohort loses.
- Universe rule (2× wrappers): decided today by the four-way test with the
  pre-committed rule; joint call on the ship.
- "Secured" on 11/15 means, all four: live within band with parity intact;
  a live monster captured; every advance via gates, zero uncontrolled
  losses; a LIVE-derived income projection.

**Phase B — commit capital (11/15/26 → mid-2027).**
- If secured: move the system to a **dedicated account** (co-mingling makes
  every parity check harder and every reconcile a manual job). Size to the
  25%-drawdown rule on whatever capital the owner dedicates.
- If one book fails its gate: it dies or drops to token size; the plan
  proceeds with the survivors. Failing honestly is a success of the process.
- Re-estimate the per-unit table from live data only, quarterly.

**Phase C — retirement math (2028+, after the 2-year floor).**
- Target income I and dedicated capital C are the owner's numbers. At
  today's honest per-unit edge, $C supports roughly I ≈ C × 3.5%/mo at the
  25%-drawdown rule (row 2: $150K → $5–7K/mo). Reaching a salary-replacing
  I means either C ≈ $250–350K or the books proving 1.5–2× the haircut
  reference live. Phase A/B tell us which.

## 5. My recommendation

Run Phase A exactly as gated, at these sizes, and do not size for income
before 11/15. The retirement plan is not "the books make $X"; it is "we know
by 11/15 which books are real, at what edge, with what drawdown, from live
data", and then capital follows evidence under a fixed drawdown rule. The
2-year floor is what makes this affordable: it buys evidence time, not
leverage.
