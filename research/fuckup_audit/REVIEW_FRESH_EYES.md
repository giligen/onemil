# Fresh-eyes review — what is MISSING between this program and real money

Written 2026-09-17 evening after reading every file under `research/fuckup_audit/` (PLAN, LOG, A–J, H/*, Q, U3, the probes, the live dumps), the two live books' own research and the lit review. Read-only; the only computation is `review/live_gaps.py` on the two live dumps. Numbers are from the files unless marked **(judgement)**.

## 0. Verdict in five lines

1. The program did ~10,000 cells of loser anatomy on **simulated** books and about four hours on the **169 real trades**. The ratio is inverted. On the real trades the things that decide P&L — execution pathology, entry hour, sizing path, config era — were never tabulated; they are larger than any "edge" the scan could have found.
2. The 116 "real ORB losers" are **115 trades under a config the owner already retired** (only 2 live trades are B+, `live_trades_dump.csv`). The loser dive is a post-mortem of a dead machine and `LIVE_LOSERS.md` does not say so.
3. The time stop (`orb_timestop_validation.md`, finished, EXIT=0) helps the **raw** population (TRAIN −0.133 → −0.056, TEST +0.022 → +0.056) and is **neutral-to-negative on the B+ book** (TRAIN +0.394 → +0.348, VAL +0.325 → +0.311, n 38/15/17). It is not "the first deployable finding"; it is a lever on trades B+ already vetoes.
4. Nothing in the program is within an order of magnitude of $10K/month at $66K. The honest portfolio arithmetic is +$1,117/month mean, worst month −$7,400 (`I/REPORT.md` §2.2). Say it to the owner in those words.
5. The only two things that can make money in the next two weeks are the two parity-audited engines (ORB B+, BF P1) with their execution fixed and the one direction the program's own cost math pointed to (multi-day R) finally run as a book.

## 3. What is structurally wrong in how the program searched

1. **Wrong unit of analysis, known in advance.** `probe_design.md` item 4 said it on 9/16: both live books are raw-negative and selection-positive (BF raw −0.01/−0.07R, ORB raw −0.18/−0.04R). The program then scored 26+14+6 raw families at first-come-alphabetical 12/4 and, when selection was finally tried (D0/D1), did it with a gradient booster on 47 features rather than the two selection stacks that had already made money. The ORB stack applied to F8 N=5 as ONE cell — the decisive test item 4 named — was never run. Ranking as a search dimension (item 11: first-four vs random-four was 100% of a prior "edge") was never pre-registered either; `H/F14_F8_F11` §4 found the booking convention alone flips a filter's sign.
2. **Width guaranteed the null.** A 192, C 1,305, D1 1,353, E ~748, G 200, H ~2,500, I 84 cells; permutation p = 0.93–1.00 on every stage because the null max-t over 100+ cells is ~3.8 while the true effects are 0.03–0.10R against MDEs of 0.06–0.25R. Then each stage wrote "no edge detectable" — correct, and useless: the design could not have detected the edge the live books run on. A professional runs one book, one hypothesis, one split; VAL of 22 weeks and TEST of 14 cannot adjudicate a 0.05R filter (H/F6 §7: VAL MDE 0.18–0.21R).
3. **The cost and fill contracts swung four times** (touch → next-open → resting → next-open; 1.90% band → 0.36% measured → contract (c) 0.25×half; Stage A: +0.41R/trade), and families were kept or dropped under different contracts. On the liquid universe the two spread tables agree (38 vs 42 bps, `J` §4) — so the whole small-cap cost fight bought nothing transferable.
4. **Specification errors kept beating the independent-rebuild rule.** ZVZZT = +50.8R of A's TEST; `BFZ_SLIP=0` pinned in every pass-1 builder; the first-break-vs-keep-scanning rule that takes F6 from +0.067/+0.215 to −0.027/−0.012 (`H/F6_reconcile` §6); all found by a third implementation, after `config.yaml` was armed for a dry run on the artefact numbers. The lesson is not "add another rule"; it is that **the engine's `detect()` is the spec** and every study must import it (J now does).
5. **Consistency bar vs tail-carried books.** Every breakout book is its tail (I §1.7: top-5% of trades = 296%/114%/305% of R; E §8.8; `probe_stops.md` §7). The owner's monthly-consistency preference is legitimate, but at 4 slots the sign of a month is a 4-sample coin flip (`bf_consistency` §3), so per-book exit tinkering cannot deliver it; only a portfolio of low-correlation sleeves can (I Part B: ρ 0.15–0.21, σ ratio 0.75) — and that arithmetic was done once, then dismissed because no sleeve was live. Capacity was measured last (`H/F6_sizing`: median trade carries $226 of risk; small-cap intraday saturates at ~$1.5–2K/month) when it should have been measured first — it bounds the whole search space.
6. **The node's cycles went to 3M-row candidate files and a 1.2M-symbol-day fetch instead of to the 169 trades and the 7,402 honest ORB fills.** The ORB honest CSV is the best dataset in the repo and got one script.
