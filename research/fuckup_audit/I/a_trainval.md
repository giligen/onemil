## 4. Where the stack's extra trades come from: added frequency vs displacement of an F6 trade

A booked trade of the stack is classified against the S0 (F6-alone) book of the same exit/split:
- **kept**: the same (day, symbol) is booked in S0 too;
- **added**: not in S0 and S0 booked FEWER than 4 trades open at that minute and < 12 that day (the slot was genuinely free);
- **displaced-in**: not in S0 and S0 was at its 4-concurrent or 12/day limit at that minute (this trade took a slot an F6 trade would have had).
