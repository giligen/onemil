## Notes (10 lines)

**Selection by Score (excluding TOM/MOM-bound strategies):** Top 3 candidates firing on **different days** from Monday (MOM) and month-end (TOM):
1. **52-Week High Momentum** (0.96, weekly any day) — oldest edge, cross-country validated
2. **Time-Series Momentum** (0.59, monthly flexible day) — asset-class agnostic, low turnover, robust mechanism
3. **Low-Vol Anomaly** (0.59, quarterly flexible) — persistent market anomaly, leveraging improves edge

**Decay warning:** Pre-FOMC (refuted 2015), PEAD (magnitude declining), Dual Momentum (underperformance since 2014), Sector Rotation (no rotation edge post-2010).
**Cost burden:** High-turnover strategies (PEAD, S&P Inclusion, Overnight) need measured NBBO per fill to pencil out.
**Data gaps:** PEAD needs earnings calendar; S&P Inclusion needs rebalance dates; Pre-FOMC needs FOMC dates (all accessible).
**Correlation strategy:** 52-Week and Time-Series both uncorrelated with MOM/TOM; pair with one calendar strategy (PEAD on earnings) for diversified stack.
**Sample ends:** Most papers 2007-2014; post-2015 evidence critical before live allocation. Turn-of-Month (robust 80+ years) and Low-Vol (persistent 2007-26) survive scrutiny.
