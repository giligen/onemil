## Rule (one implementation shared by BT and live, `research/sleeves/fiftytwo_wh.py` → later `scripts/`)
* Universe: point-in-time Databento EQUS.SUMMARY daily bars incl. delisted (`data/research/databento`, accessor
  `research/scripts/pit_listings.py`), common stocks only, price ≥ $5, 60-day median dollar volume ≥ $5M, no wrappers
  (`^Z[A-Z]ZZT$` and the 2x/3x list excluded by name), membership knowable at the signal close.
* Signal on the LAST trading day of each month at the close: `ratio = close / max(high over the prior 252 sessions)`.
  Rank; buy the top 30 names by ratio (equal weight) that are NOT already held; hold each name 3 months (3 overlapping
  monthly cohorts → ~90 positions max at 1/90 each, sleeve $10K → ~$110/name: too small). **Our variant, frozen:** top
  20 names, hold 1 month, full rebalance monthly — single cohort, 20 × $500. (Report the published 30 × 6-month variant
  as a sensitivity on the same data; the sleeve runs the frozen variant.)
* Orders: market-on-open the first session of the month (BT buys/sells at the official open, as the MOM sleeve does);
  the live script submits at 09:46 ET like MOM — paper-vs-live slip is measured, not assumed.
* Cost in BT: measured per-trade NBBO half-spread at the open from our 1-min data where available, else the
  minute-of-day table is NOT valid at the open — use 15 bps round trip as the frozen placeholder and report sensitivity
  at 30 bps. Turnover ≈ 100 %/month → cost ≈ 0.3–0.6 %/mo: the edge must clear it.
* Trigger day: first session of each month (≠ MOM Monday, ≠ TOM month-end entry — TOM exits the same days; report the
  overlap of the two sleeves' positions and the shared-tail correlation of monthly returns).
