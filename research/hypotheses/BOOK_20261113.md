## Self-improving loop (the protocol, binding from cell 1,752)
1. Every RESULT_<cell>.md ends with "CHILDREN:" — ≥ 2 new hypotheses written FROM the winners-vs-losers table
   (what the winners had that the losers did not, as a causal feature), each with family, parent, data, kill rule.
2. Children enter this book the same day; the book is re-ranked (gross × frequency × cost survival × data on disk).
3. Three dead children in a family → the family is retired with one line saying what killed it.
4. One frozen cell at a time; one VAL read per cell; TEST sealed; independent rebuild before any PASS reaches the
   owner. Cheap cells (data on disk, < 500 MB) run during RTH; heavy ones after 20:05 UTC.
5. The owner sees this file, not the cells.
