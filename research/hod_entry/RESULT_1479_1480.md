## Cost model (both cells) — the PREREG's own ambiguity, resolved and disclosed here
Neither cell's prose gives closed-form cost algebra for a brand-new exit point with no fresh NBBO measurement.
This build: (1) recovers REAL half_entry (HE) via `cell_1445.corrected_cost()` and REAL exit_half (EH) via the
exact algebraic identity `EH = cost_R*R − HE − SLIP_BP*exit_price` (no 50/50 proxy — a genuine improvement over
the pre-amendment disclosed sample, which lacked access to `cell_1445.py`'s own machinery); (2) "charge HE again"
for the pyramid add is applied on the ADD'S OWN SHARES (2/3 N), which telescopes to the SAME total entry cost as
one full-size trade once added, and to exactly 1/3 of it if never added — a single consistent share-weighted rule,
not two different ad hoc formulas; (3) the verified stop-limit exit slip (RESULT_1463_unbiased.md's own fill/no-fill
counts: TRAIN-H2 15.57 bps, VAL 10.91 bps blended) is applied only to this cell's own NEW stop-type exits, since
`bars_fills_1478.db` carries no tick data to re-measure a fresh stop-limit fill at the moved (breakeven) stop.
This is a testable, disclosed choice, not a silent guess — flagged for owner/reviewer sign-off, not shipped as fact.
