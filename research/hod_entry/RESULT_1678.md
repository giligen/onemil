## Part 3 -- ORB transfer

**Coverage: 478/483 entered ORB fills usable (99.0%)** after backfilling 382 missing (symbol,day) pairs (209,465 bars, 231 days) into `bars_sip.db` via the scratchpad wrapper; 5 lost to still-missing bars. **Reconstruction caveat (load-bearing, read before the numbers below):** `orb_bplus_book.csv` carries entry_price, pnl_pct and exit_reason but NOT entry_time/stop/exit_time (confirmed against `research/exec_cost/compute.py`'s own caveat: "ORB honest book carries no entry_time/stop/shares"). This script reconstructs stop = range_low of the first 5 RTH minutes (the documented RULE, `research/orb_machine_rules.md` L8), entry bar = first bar >=09:35 ET touching entry_price, exit_price = entry_price*(1+pnl_pct/100), exit bar = first bar touching exit_price in the exit_reason-implied direction (EOD-type reasons -> the EOD bar). **This is a reconstruction, not the live engine's static-lock/touchgo state machine or a real timestamp ledger.**

Own-book give-back anatomy (on the reconstructed timing): 256/478 fills (53.6%) reached >=+1R at some point; **21.3% of ALL 478 fills reached >=+1R and closed <=0** (a full give-back), losing a mean 1.88R from peak to exit among that ever-1R population.

Applying the Part 2 HOD-trained remaining-R models UNCHANGED (both halves' models, ORB's own R units), rule X(c)/X+ from minute 15 to exit: **mechanically clears the PREREG pass bar (dR>=+0.05R, t>=2.0, ex-top5%>0) on all 12 (featset x c x variant) cells, both HOD-trained models** -- mean dR +0.53R to +0.72R for X (day_t 3.8-6.5, n_fired 36-54 of n_pool=78) and +1.40R to +1.72R for X+ (day_t 6.8-16.1, n_fired 6-11 of **n_pool=17**); ex-top-5% stays positive throughout (+0.46 to +1.65R); dollar effect at the $375 live risk = $200-$644/fired trade. **Do not read this as a validated edge:** (a) n_pool=78/478 (16%) for X and just **17/478 (3.6%) for X+** -- the rule almost never fires on this book; (b) the reconstructed own_net_R baseline has a heavy right tail (max 11.6R, 8/478 fills >5R, e.g. ANTX 2026-03-09 at 11.6R) consistent with the documented "R must exceed the spread" pitfall (a tight reconstructed range_low shrinks R_unit and inflates R-normalized outcomes for that fill); (c) entry/exit timing is reconstructed, not ledger-real. Per the PREREG's own pass-bar clause, this is **a mechanical pass that requires an independent rebuild from a real trade-level ledger before it is a claim**, let alone before ORB PAPER. All 24 rows in `1678_orb_reads.csv`; per-fill detail in `1678_orb_per_fill.csv`.
