## F1 stale-trade / time x progress

**H1 — exit at 30 min if mtm < +0.25 R**
Fires on ~49% of the book (TRAIN 48.7%, VAL 49.5%) — roughly half of all fills are still under +0.25R twenty-nine minutes in. dR is small and sign-flips (TRAIN -0.0225R, t_day -0.68; VAL -0.0052R, t_day +0.11): no significant edge. Decomposition: 60-61% of fired trades land in the win-side "saved" bucket (mean +0.76/+0.77R) but the 39-40% forgone bucket averages -1.24 to -1.29R, ~1.6x larger per trade, so total forgone dollars exceed total saved dollars in both reads (cost negligible at 0.0006R). Consistency lens is net negative: green-week share falls 11.1pp TRAIN (44%→33%) and is flat on VAL; weekly P10 improves TRAIN (+2.3) but worsens VAL (-5.7). WHY: 30 minutes is early relative to this population's typical path — more than half the eventual winners haven't shown +0.25R yet, so the rule's 60% win-side hit rate is real but each wrong cut gives back 1.6x what each right cut saves.

**H2 — exit at 60 min if mtm < +0.5 R**
Fires less often (36.4% TRAIN, 38.4% VAL) — the extra 30 minutes and higher +0.5R bar let more trades "prove themselves" before judgment. dR is close to flat and sign-flips: TRAIN -0.0138R (t_day -1.81, the family's largest-magnitude clustered t) vs VAL +0.0011R (t_day +0.30), where sum_saved (+538.7) and sum_forgone (-535.3) almost exactly cancel. Decomposition: saved-bucket share rises to ~58%, mean +0.74/+0.77R, against a forgone mean of -1.04 to -1.13R (~1.5x) — the same lopsided per-trade story as H1, just touching fewer trades. Consistency: green-week share drops 7.4pp TRAIN, flat VAL; weekly P10 improves slightly both reads; maxdd shrinks 25-35%. WHY: this is a weaker, noisier version of H1's mechanism — waiting longer narrows the per-trade damage enough to reach breakeven on VAL but doesn't flip the sign on TRAIN.

**H3 — exit at 90 min if mtm < +0.75 R**
The most conservative trigger in the family: fires on only 30.4% (TRAIN) / 32.5% (VAL) of fills, the lowest of H1-H5. dR is the flattest of the group (TRAIN -0.0049R t_day -1.22, VAL -0.0018R t_day +0.14) — essentially indistinguishable from the base rule on the whole book. Decomposition: saved/forgone means are closest to parity here (+0.77 vs -0.96R, a 1.24x ratio vs 1.6-1.8x for H1/H4) because the extra time and higher bar filter more "about to turn" trades out of the forgone bucket before they fire. Consistency is F1's one bright spot: green-week share IMPROVES on both reads (+3.7pp TRAIN, +9.1pp VAL), though weekly P10 is mixed (+0.5 TRAIN, -4.0 VAL) and maxdd shrinks 30-40% in both. WHY: giving stale trades the most room of the family removes most of the false-positive stalls, so what's left fires closer to true dead trades — no R edge, but the closest thing to a free consistency gain in F1.

**H4 — >20 min since the last new high AND mtm < +0.5 R**
The broadest trigger in F1: fires on 51.3% (TRAIN) / 51.8% (VAL) of the book — "20 minutes without a new high" is a common early pause most trades pass through, not a rare stall. dR is the second-most negative of the family (TRAIN -0.0242R, VAL -0.0047R; t_day -0.55/+0.10, both non-significant) despite having the highest saved-bucket share (61-62%, mean +0.72/+0.76R), because the forgone bucket (38-39% of fires, mean -1.22 to -1.29R) runs 1.6-1.8x larger per trade — tied with H1 for the worst per-trade asymmetry. Consistency is the worst in F1: green-week share falls on BOTH reads (-11.1pp TRAIN, -9.1pp VAL), the only H1-H5 rule to do so on both halves, even though weekly P10 (+5.2/+0.8) and maxdd both improve. WHY: the stall signal is a false-positive machine — most 20-minute new-high droughts in this population resolve upward, so the rule trades away green weeks for a tail-protection benefit that doesn't show up in whole-book R.

**H5 — after +1 R, exit if no new high in 15 min**
Fires on 32.1% (TRAIN) / 31.6% (VAL) of fills — it only engages once a trade has already banked +1R, so it touches a narrower, higher-mtm slice than H1-H4. It is F1's worst performer: dR -0.0346R TRAIN (t_day -0.78; iid t -2.41, the only individually "significant" naive t in the family, wrong-signed) and -0.0166R VAL (t_day -0.35); ex-top-5% is the most negative of the five (-0.13/-0.11R), so this is not a tail artifact. Decomposition breaks the family pattern: only 44.4% (TRAIN) / 45.8% (VAL) of fired trades land in the saved bucket (vs 55-61% for H1-H4) — the majority of fires are continuation-forgone — even though the per-trade saved mean (+1.06 to +1.09R) is the largest in F1 (protecting a banked +1R pays well when right). Consistency is the worst of all: green-week share falls 14.8pp TRAIN / 13.6pp VAL, and maxdd WORSENS on TRAIN (-146→-154), the only F1 rule to make drawdown worse. WHY: after +1R, a 15-minute lull is a normal part of this population's path to a bigger winner far more often than it's the start of a reversal, so exiting on it is wrong on the majority of fires and each miss is expensive.

## F2 shape story line (rolling candles)

**H6 — bearish engulfing 5m candle while >=+0.5R**
Fires on 26.4% (TRAIN) / 28.3% (VAL) of the book once a trade has cleared +0.5R -- a fairly common candle pattern at that stage. dR is small and negative both reads (TRAIN -0.0081R, day_t -0.67; VAL -0.0053R, day_t +0.04, neither significant) with ex-top-5% notably worse (-0.105/-0.103R), showing the whole-book number is propped up by a handful of large saved trades rather than a real effect. Decomposition: 59% (TRAIN) / 57% (VAL) of fires land in the forgone bucket (mean -0.90/-0.88R) vs 41%/43% saved (mean +1.23/+1.15R) -- fire COUNT, not per-trade size, drives the small net loss; cost is negligible (0.0006R). Consistency is regime-dependent: green-week share +7.4pp and maxdd shrinks 29% on TRAIN, but VAL reverses (-9.1pp green weeks, weekly P10 -0.2R, sharpe worse both reads). WHY: a bearish engulfing candle above +0.5R is ambiguous -- slightly more often a genuine top than a shakeout by count, but the margin is too thin and sample-dependent to trust; the TRAIN-side consistency gain does not replicate on VAL.

**H7 — close below trailing 10m low while >=+0.5R**
Fires on 22.4% (TRAIN) / 25.2% (VAL), the tightest non-trivial gate in F2 -- breaking a 10-minute low is a real but not rare structural break once a trade is above +0.5R. dR is consistently negative (TRAIN -0.0090R day_t -0.77; VAL -0.0150R day_t -0.55, the most negative day-clustered VAL t outside H11) and ex-top-5% is similar magnitude to raw dR (-0.103/-0.106R), so unlike H6 this is not purely a tail-cushioned illusion. Decomposition: forgone dominates both by count (58-59% of fires) and combined R (sum -278/-406R vs saved sum +257/+359R) -- the 10-minute-low break catches more continuing trades than real reversals. Consistency: green-week +3.7pp TRAIN but -13.6pp VAL (the family's worst VAL green-week hit), weekly P10 mixed (+2.1/-2.7R), maxdd improves more on TRAIN than VAL. WHY: a 10-minute low is short enough that ordinary pullback noise breaks it constantly in a live uptrend -- the rule is cutting the population's normal continuation pattern, not its reversals.

**H8 — 5m close below day VWAP while >=+0.5R**
Essentially inert on this population: fires on just 0.5% (TRAIN, n=12) / 0.8% (VAL, n=24) of fills -- once a HOD-break trade is already +0.5R above entry, a 5-minute close back under day VWAP is a rare event, not a common failure mode. dR is ~0 both reads (TRAIN -0.0002R, VAL +0.0005R) and ex-top-5% is likewise ~0 (-0.0015/-0.0024R) -- there is no whole-book signal to explain because the rule barely touches the book. Decomposition at n=12/24 is pure noise (saved mean +0.58/+0.64R vs forgone -0.49/-0.73R, opposite-leaning shares between reads); cost 0.0006R. The consistency lens shows no detectable move: green-week share unchanged both reads, weekly P10 flat TRAIN / -0.4R VAL, maxdd nudges better by under 6R. WHY: by the time a HOD-break trade is +0.5R above entry, day VWAP is usually already far below price -- this trigger condition is structurally too rare post-entry to matter; it is a non-event on this population, not a real candidate.

**H9 — upper-wick rejection (wick>=2x body) while >=+1R**
Gated at the highest profit bar in F2 (+1R), fires on 30.0% (TRAIN) / 31.2% (VAL) -- a wick-at-least-double-the-body rejection candle is common once a trade is running. dR flips sign (TRAIN +0.0008R day_t -0.40; VAL -0.0151R day_t -0.38) and ex-top-5% is the most negative in the family (-0.114/-0.126R), so the TRAIN point estimate is entirely a tail artifact. Decomposition is F2's most lopsided by count: only 32% of fires land in saved (mean +1.46/+1.31R, the largest per-trade win in F2 -- protecting a real reversal after +1R pays well when right) against 68% forgone (mean -0.68/-0.69R); sum_saved (+328/+412R) barely beats sum_forgone (-326/-459R) on TRAIN and loses on VAL. Consistency: green-week +3.7pp TRAIN / -13.6pp VAL (identical hit to H7), weekly P10 +4.5/-1.4R, sharpe worse both reads. WHY: an upper-wick rejection after +1R is usually the trade breathing on the way to a bigger winner -- two of every three fires give up a continuation, and only the rare true-top case pays enough to nearly cover them.

**H10 — climax bar (vol>=3x mean, CLV<=0.5) at any profit**
The only F2 rule with no minimum-R gate -- fires on 29.9% (TRAIN) / 31.4% (VAL) of the whole book, at any profit level. dR is the family's only clean double-positive: +0.0044R TRAIN / +0.0014R VAL, though day-clustered t is small and sign-inconsistent (-0.59/+0.38) and ex-top-5% is still negative (-0.088/-0.087R) -- a real but weak, tail-assisted effect, not yet a signal. Decomposition is the most balanced in F2: 47-48% saved (mean +1.05/+1.04R) vs 52-53% forgone (mean -0.92/-0.92R), sums nearly cancel (+351/-341R TRAIN, +487/-483R VAL) -- a volume-climax-with-weak-close is a genuine coin-flip on this population. Consistency is the family's clearest tension: green-week share WORSENS both reads (-7.4/-9.1pp) while weekly P10 (+5.3/+1.1R) and maxdd (+48/+46R) both improve markedly. WHY: climax-bar exits cut true blow-off tops and mid-run volume spikes about equally often, so the book's average R edges up slightly and its worst weeks get shallower, but ordinary green weeks lose a few winners that would have carried them over the line.

**H11 — trailing 15m candle turns red after +1R**
F2's broadest post-+1R trigger: fires on 34.3% (TRAIN) / 34.6% (VAL), the highest fired share in the family. dR is by far the worst in F2 (TRAIN -0.0404R -- the family's only naive-significant iid_t at -2.62, though day_t is a more modest -0.93; VAL -0.0167R day_t -0.12) and ex-top-5% is the most negative of all (-0.138/-0.113R). Decomposition: 55-56% of fires land in forgone (mean -1.13/-1.00R) vs 44-45% saved (mean +1.15/+1.12R) -- close to balanced per-trade, but the extra forgone count combined with the extra 15 minutes of exposure this rule requires (the slowest-to-confirm trigger in F2) produces the family's largest sum_forgone (-506/-603R). Consistency is the worst in F2: green-week share collapses -18.5pp TRAIN / -18.2pp VAL (both halves, the only F2 rule to hit this hard on both), sharpe worsens most severely of the family (-0.33 TRAIN), and maxdd is the only F2 case to WORSEN on TRAIN (-146R to -163R). WHY: a single 15-minute candle turning red after +1R is this population's normal rhythm, not a reversal tell -- it fires often enough and late enough that it reliably clips the runners that make a week green.

**H12 — two consecutive lower 5m highs while >=+0.5R**
Fires on 29.6% (TRAIN) / 32.4% (VAL), a moderately common early-stall pattern above +0.5R. dR is small and negative both reads (TRAIN -0.0122R day_t -0.37; VAL -0.0115R day_t +0.20 -- sign-inconsistent clustered t despite near-identical point estimates) with ex-top-5% notably worse (-0.110/-0.109R), again showing the small net loss undercounts the typical trade. Decomposition: 58% of fires land in forgone both reads (mean -0.96/-0.91R) vs 42% saved (mean +1.22/+1.15R) -- the same count-driven asymmetry as H6/H7/H9, two lower highs is common enough in genuine continuation that it misfires more than it protects. Consistency: green-week +3.7pp TRAIN / -13.6pp VAL, weekly P10 improves both reads (+2.5/+1.2R), maxdd improves both (+40/+33R). WHY: "two lower highs" is a shape that shows up constantly in noisy uptrends, so like H6/H7/H9 it trades away more continuation than it protects -- the identical -13.6pp VAL hit recurring across H7, H9 and H12 suggests these three triggers clip the same handful of green VAL weeks, not three independent effects.

## F3 model-based

**H13 — P(+1R next15)<0.3 while >=+0.5R (rolling from k=5)**
Fires on 15.8% (TRAIN) / 17.2% (VAL) of the book once mtm clears +0.5R and the model rates <30% odds of reaching +1R in the next 15 minutes. dR is ~flat both reads (-0.0008R / -0.0182R) with iid_t/day_t sign-flipping across reads (day_t +0.92 TRAIN, -1.39 VAL) and ex5 more negative than raw dR both times (-0.092/-0.114) -- the small edge leans on the tail. Decomposition: forgone edges out saved by SUM in both reads (TRAIN -304.65R over 289 fires vs +302.19R over 211; VAL -276.23R/246 vs +233.51R/159) even though the per-trade saved mean (+1.43/+1.47R) beats |forgone| mean (-1.05/-1.12R) -- it's the 58-61% fire-share toward forgone, not trade size, that tips it slightly negative. Consistency: green-week flat (0pp both reads), weekly P10 improves (+3.03R/+2.94R) and maxdd shrinks (-28R/-12R), but Sharpe worsens both reads -- a tail-insurance shape, not a return-adder. WHY: the rolling P<0.3 gate fires more often on trades merely pausing than truly topping, so it trades away more continuation-count than it protects, even though the rare "protects" case is individually the bigger trade.

**H14 — P(stop after k)>0.7 while >=+0.25R**
Fires on 14.5% (TRAIN) / 11.1% (VAL). dR is consistently negative (-0.0133R/-0.0205R, iid_t -1.38/-2.11) and forgone dominates by BOTH count (52.8%/53.8% of fires) and per-trade size (mean -1.38/-1.42R vs saved +1.35/+1.26R) -- sum_forgone (-333R/-199R) beats sum_saved (+291R/+151R) by the widest margin of the family's binary gates. Consistency is the worst trade-off in F3's de-risk cells: green-week share drops hard (-4.5pp TRAIN, -11.1pp VAL) while weekly P10 (+4.52R/+1.40R) and maxdd (+12R/~flat) only partly compensate, and Sharpe worsens most of the binary triggers. WHY: "P(stop)>0.7" measures downside risk, not upside exhaustion, so it fires on trades that are still structurally fine setups more often than H13's continuation-probability gate -- cutting them early converts real green weeks to flat/red for a smaller tail-risk payoff than H13 bought.

**H15 — H13's and H14's conditions both hold**
Fires on only 1.9% (TRAIN, n=59) / 1.1% (VAL, n=27) -- intersecting two already-narrow gates leaves too few trades to read. dR is ~0 both reads (-0.0051R/-0.0006R), t-stats near zero and sign-flipping (day_t -1.09/+0.12); forgone still edges saved by sum (TRAIN -43.67R/37 fires vs +27.44R/22; VAL -17.69R/16 vs +16.20R/11, the gap nearly closing on VAL). Consistency lens is essentially silent: green-week unchanged on VAL (0pp), +4.5pp on TRAIN off a tiny base, weekly P10 and maxdd barely move (+0.55R/+0.09R). WHY: this cell is underpowered by construction (~1-2% fired share both reads) -- it cannot distinguish "the AND combination helps" from noise and needs a far larger population before it says anything.

**H16 — the 1,677 reference: k>=60, mtm>=+1R, P<0.3**
The family's rarest and only cleanly-gated-positive trigger: fires on just 1.4% (TRAIN, n=43) / 1.8% (VAL, n=42) -- a single fixed 60-bar checkpoint, not rolling. dR is positive both reads (+0.0041R/+0.0024R, reproducing the 1677 reference per the harness build validation), and here SAVED dominates -- not by count (48.8%/40.5% of fires, a minority) but by size: mean saved (+1.39R/+1.50R) is roughly double |mean forgone| (-0.74R/-0.79R), so sum_saved (+29.3R/+25.5R) clears sum_forgone (-16.2R/-19.8R) despite fewer fires. ex5 stays negative both reads (-0.005/-0.009) and day_t collapses read-to-read (2.02 TRAIN -> 0.04 VAL), so the positive point estimate leans on the tail and doesn't replicate as a day-independent effect. Consistency is flat-to-mildly-good everywhere (green day/week ~0pp, small P10/Sharpe/maxdd gains) simply because 1.4-1.8% can't move a whole book. WHY: late + strict (mtm>=1R, P<0.3) selects genuinely exhausted trades where being right pays about 2x being wrong, but the trigger is too rare and too tail-dependent to matter at the book level as-is -- a candidate to narrow and size up, not to ship broad.

**H17 — P-weighted partial at +1R: sell round((1-P)x4)/4 in quarters**
By far the most damaging F3 rule and the only one touching a large slice of the book: fires on 11.1% (TRAIN) / 12.9% (VAL) -- because it triggers on essentially every trade that reaches +1R except the rare P>0.875 high-conviction case, so its population overlaps most +1R-reachers, not a filtered subset like H16. dR is large, negative and the most internally consistent result in the family (-0.0469R/-0.0499R, iid_t -9.37/-8.04, day_t -8.21/-7.17 -- iid and day-clustered agree in sign and rough size, so this is not a within-day-clustering artifact), and ex5 is even more negative than raw dR (-0.067/-0.074), confirming the loss is broad-based, not tail-driven. Decomposition is the most lopsided in F3: 79.3% (TRAIN) / 76.3% (VAL) of fires land in forgone (sum -199.85R/-164.97R) against 20.7%/23.7% saved (sum +51.84R/+47.70R) -- quantizing the partial to round((1-P)x4)/4 still forces a real sale on any trade the model isn't extremely confident about, repeatedly shaving size off winners that go on to run. Consistency collapses hardest in F3: green-week -18.2pp/-11.1pp, weekly P10 WORSENS (-3.89R/-4.63R, the only F3 rule to make the worst weeks worse), Sharpe and maxdd both deteriorate sharply. WHY: a conviction-weighted partial sounds well-calibrated, but because it fires on nearly all +1R trades and the quantization rounds even moderate conviction up to a 25-50% sale, it systematically under-sizes the population's real continuation -- this is the family's clearest "close it" result, not an iterate-further one.

## F4 partials / scale-outs

**H18 — 50% out at +1R, rest as the live rule**
Fires on 45.5% (TRAIN, n=1068) / 45.4% (VAL, n=1434) -- the same >=+1R population as H19 and H21. dR is ~flat both reads (TRAIN +0.0002R iid_t 0.03 day_t 0.08; VAL -0.0036R iid_t -0.50 day_t 0.62) but ex5 is clearly negative both times (-0.063/-0.064), so even the flat point estimate leans on a thin tail. Decomposition: saved is 34% of fires both reads (mean +0.79R/+0.73R, sum +288/+359R) vs forgone 66% (mean -0.41R/-0.40R, sum -285/-374R) -- the sums nearly cancel TRAIN and tip slightly negative VAL, driven by the 2:1 count split rather than trade size; 24% (TRAIN) / 23% (VAL) of fired trades finish negative under the base rule despite having touched +1R, the real giveback this partial targets. Consistency: green-day +0.8pp/-1.0pp, green-week flat/-4.5pp, weekly P10 improves both reads (+5.0R/+3.6R), sharpe worsens both (-0.02/-0.05), maxdd shallower both (+39.9R/+29.2R). WHY: halving size the instant +1R is touched is a pure variance cut -- it shrinks every fired trade's swing in half regardless of where it was headed, buying P10/maxdd relief in both reads for near-zero net R, but on VAL it costs green weeks because half of the would-be week-carrying runners are now only half-sized.

**H19 — 33% at +1R, 33% at +2R, rest trails MFE-1R**
Same >=+1R population as H18 (fired 45.5%/45.4%, n=1068/1434), cut more gently. dR is F4's smallest in magnitude both reads (TRAIN +0.0023R iid_t 0.36 day_t 0.35; VAL -0.0010R iid_t -0.19 day_t 0.79) and ex5 is also F4's least negative (-0.042/-0.043) -- scaling out in thirds visibly softens both tails of H18's trade. Decomposition mirrors H18's 34%/66% saved/forgone split but at roughly two-thirds the per-trade size on each side (saved mean +0.55R/+0.50R, forgone -0.27R/-0.26R), since only a third is locked at +1R versus H18's half. Consistency: green-week +3.7pp TRAIN (F4's cleanest single TRAIN gain) / flat VAL, weekly P10 +3.1R/+2.6R, sharpe ~flat TRAIN (-0.005) / -0.03 VAL, maxdd +30.6R/+23.2R. WHY: thirds-at-1R/2R-then-trail-MFE-1R is H18's mechanism diluted -- same fired population, same direction, smaller magnitude on every axis; it is the one F4 cell whose TRAIN consistency gain isn't paired with a VAL give-back, though VAL itself is flat rather than positive.

**H20 — 50% out at +1R only if P(+1R next15)<0.5**
A model-gated version of H18 that cuts the touched population to 6.2% (TRAIN-H2->VAL, n=196) / 6.6% (VAL->TRAIN-H2, n=154) -- roughly 7x narrower than the unconditional +1R gate. dR is F4's largest and most internally consistent: +0.0057R (iid_t 1.68, day_t 1.70) and +0.0072R (iid_t 1.64, day_t 1.61) -- the only F4 cell where iid and day-clustered t agree in sign and both clear 1.6 on both reads; ex5 is also F4's least negative (-0.017/-0.019). Decomposition keeps H18's shape (saved 34%/33% of fires, mean +0.83R/+0.80R; forgone 66%/67%, mean -0.38R/-0.37R) -- the model filter narrows reach, not the per-trade split. Consistency: green-week +4.5pp/+3.7pp (F4's best pair), weekly P10 +1.0R one direction / -0.2R the other (F4's only read where P10 doesn't improve), sharpe IMPROVES both reads (+0.03/+0.03, the only F4 cell where this happens), maxdd +9.8R/+20.3R (smallest maxdd gain in F4, consistent with touching the fewest trades). WHY: gating the same +1R partial on the model's own low-continuation read is F4's standout cell -- narrower reach but the only one where day-clustered t and weekly Sharpe both move the right way on both reads; still short of a conventional significance bar, and the muted P10 gain shows it isn't doing H18/H22's tail-insurance job -- its value is selectivity, not size.

**H21 — 50% out at +1R and stop to breakeven for the rest**
Same +1R population as H18/H19 (fired 45.5%/45.4%, n=1068/1434) with the most aggressive follow-through: a breakeven stop on the running half. dR is F4's worst both reads (-0.0128R iid_t -0.98 day_t -0.49; -0.0119R iid_t -1.13 day_t 0.10, sign-inconsistent clustered t) and ex5 is F4's most negative (-0.102/-0.098). Decomposition: saved mean is F4's LARGEST (+1.13R/+1.07R, sum +390/+483R) because a breakeven stop prevents the worst round-trips outright rather than trimming them, but forgone also carries F4's largest per-trade cost (-0.58R/-0.53R) and highest count-share (68%/69% of fires, sum -417/-524R) -- ordinary intraday noise shakes the breakeven stop out of trades that would have continued. Consistency: green-day +3.1pp/-3.9pp, green-week flat/-9.1pp (F4's worst green-week hit), weekly P10 +3.2R/+3.5R, sharpe worsens most of any F4 cell (-0.12/-0.11), maxdd improves most (+37.8R/+38.1R). WHY: breakeven-stopping the runner buys F4's best per-trade rescue and best maxdd relief by cutting off round-trips at the root, but it is over-tightened -- the same mechanism that prevents the worst losers also stops out ordinary continuation noise on good trades, converting more green weeks to flat/red than any other F4 rule.

**H22 — 25% out at +0.5R, rest as the live rule**
The lowest bar and broadest reach in F4: fires on 62.3% (TRAIN, n=1463) / 60.9% (VAL, n=1922) of the book. dR is small both reads (-0.0006R iid_t -0.10 day_t 0.23; -0.0036R iid_t -0.72 day_t 0.81) with ex5 tied with H20 for F4's least negative (-0.032/-0.032). Decomposition is F4's most balanced by count: 46%/45% saved (mean +0.36R/+0.34R) vs 54%/55% forgone (mean -0.30R/-0.29R) -- the closest to a coin flip in the family, since at only +0.5R of confirmation "about to round-trip" and "about to keep running" are nearly equally likely. 40% (TRAIN) / 38% (VAL) of fired trades finish negative under the base rule (vs 23-24% for the +1R-gated rules), confirming this shallower population carries more genuine giveback risk to begin with. Consistency: green-day -0.8pp/-2.0pp, green-week flat/-4.5pp, weekly P10 +3.6R TRAIN / +5.3R VAL (F4's single best P10 improvement), sharpe worsens both (-0.02/-0.04), maxdd +31.8R/+27.3R. WHY: trading a quarter off at only +0.5R touches more trades than any other F4 rule and buys the family's best tail relief because it disarms round-trips earliest, but that early cut clips enough small leaders to cost green weeks on VAL even though net R stays roughly flat.

## F5 target / stop reshaping

**H23 — target cap to +1.5R when the trailing 30-min candle is red (CLV<0.5)**
Fires broadly and early, independent of profit level: 51.7% (TRAIN, n=1214) / 53.1% (VAL, n=1677) of the WHOLE book, not gated by any R threshold -- fired trades average base_R near breakeven (+0.06/+0.07) vs unfired's -0.11/-0.15, so it engages on winners and losers alike off a single 30-min candle read. dR is small negative both reads (-0.0070R iid_t -0.93 day_t -1.37; -0.0031R iid_t -0.46 day_t -0.90), ex5 slightly more negative than raw dR (-0.066/-0.067). Decomposition: saved wins by COUNT (754/1214, 1022/1677 fires, 61-62%) but forgone wins by size (-0.32/-0.31R per fill vs saved's +0.17/+0.19R) -- forgone sum (-147R/-201R) edges out saved sum (+131R/+191R) both reads. Consistency: green-week flat TRAIN / -4.5pp VAL, weekly P10 WORSENS both reads (-0.39R/-4.14R) -- the only F5 cell where P10 gets worse, not better. WHY: capping the target on a single weak-candle read fires on roughly half the book regardless of how the trade is doing, so its rare large give-backs (the true late-day 1.5R+ runners it caps) outweigh its frequent small saves, and because it also fires on trades that haven't earned any cushion yet it drags the worst weeks down instead of cushioning them.

**H24 — target cap to +3R when P(+1R next 15m)>0.7 at the +1R mark (model)**
The family's narrowest and only model-gated cell: fires on 19.0% (TRAIN->VAL, n=601) / 16.7% (VAL->TRAIN, n=393) -- requires a SUCCESS_KS checkpoint AND close_R>=1.0 AND psucc>0.7, so it only ever touches trades already at/above +1R (fired base_R avg +1.29/+1.28). dR is the only consistently positive point estimate in F5 (+0.0016R iid_t 1.16 day_t 0.74; +0.0024R iid_t 1.35 day_t 1.25), though neither clears a conventional bar, and ex5 is ~0 both reads (-0.0004/-0.0002) -- unlike every other F5 cell this one does not lean on a tail. Decomposition is lopsided toward forgone by count (82%/81% of fires) but forgone per-fill is tiny (-0.002R) while saved per-fill is real (+0.057R/+0.083R), so sum_saved (+6.1R/+6.3R) clears sum_forgone (-1.1R/-0.6R) easily -- capping a high-conviction runner at 3R rarely costs anything because the model is usually right that it keeps going past 3R anyway. Consistency: green-week flat both reads, weekly P10 +0.24R/-0.01R (~flat), sharpe and maxdd both improve slightly. WHY: gating the reshape on the model's own psucc>0.7 at the +1R checkpoint is the one F5 mechanism selective enough (17-19%, already-winning trades only) that its rare misses cost almost nothing -- the one F5 cell worth a second look, though the edge itself is too small (dR ~0.002R, t<1.4) to ship alone.

**H25 — stop to breakeven at +1R (exit-lab X5 replicate)**
Fires on 48.2% (TRAIN, n=1132) / 48.1% (VAL, n=1517) -- any trade whose HIGH reaches +1R, regardless of where it closes; fired base_R averages +0.98 both reads (these are trades that were genuinely working), and whole-book dR (-0.0215R/-0.0119R) exact-matches the harness build's validation against the prior exit-lab's -0.017R/-0.018R. Decomposition is the clearest "forgone wins by volume" case in F5: forgone is 73-75% of fires (830/1132, 1132/1517) vs saved's 25-27%, and even though each saved fill recoups more (+0.92R/+0.90R) than each forgone fill costs (-0.40R/-0.34R), the 3:1 count gap flips it -- sum_forgone (-328R/-385R) beats sum_saved (+278R/+347R) both reads. Consistency is the starkest trade-off in F5: green-week drops hard (-7.4pp TRAIN, -13.6pp VAL) while weekly P10 barely moves (+1.43R/+0.12R) and maxdd improves substantially (+11R/+34R) -- sharpe worsens both reads. WHY: ordinary intraday noise re-tests breakeven on roughly 3 of every 4 trades that touch +1R, so "lock in no-loss" mostly just stops out trades that would have continued to the live target -- it trims some catastrophic weeks (maxdd improves) but converts far more marginal green weeks to flat/red than it rescues, matching the prior exit-lab finding almost exactly.

**H26 — stop to +0.5R at +1.5R**
Fires on 35.2% (TRAIN, n=827) / 34.6% (VAL, n=1091) -- a stricter version of H25 gated at +1.5R instead of +1R (fired base_R avg +1.43/+1.44, all genuine runners). dR is small negative both reads (-0.0065R iid_t -0.68 day_t -0.43; -0.0098R iid_t -1.26 day_t -0.57), ex5 more negative than raw (-0.079/-0.075). Decomposition: forgone dominates by count even harder than H25 (84-85% of fires vs H25's 73-75%) though saved per-fill is F5's largest (+1.26R/+1.14R, locking +0.5R this late saves more when it does save) -- sum_forgone (-177R/-227R) still beats sum_saved (+162R/+196R) both reads. Consistency: green-day improves TRAIN (+3.1pp) but green-week still falls (-3.7pp/-9.1pp), weekly P10 improves TRAIN sharply (+3.12R) but WORSENS VAL (-0.93R) -- the only sign-flip in F5's tail effect -- sharpe worsens both, maxdd improves both. WHY: raising the bar to +1.5R before locking +0.5R selects trades that have already proven themselves more than H25's +1R gate, so individual saves are bigger, but it still fires on 5-of-6 "forgone" trades that were only pausing before continuing, and the P10 sign-flip between TRAIN/VAL means this cell's tail benefit isn't stable, not just its point estimate.

**H27 — trail to the trailing 10-min candle's low once run-up hits +1R**
Fires on 48.2% (TRAIN, n=1133) / 48.1% (VAL, n=1517) -- same run_hi_R>=1.0 gate as H25 (fired base_R avg +0.98 both reads), but trails a moving floor instead of a fixed breakeven. dR is F5's most negative point estimate (-0.0326R TRAIN, iid_t -2.08 but day_t only -0.69 -- a clean illustration of why day-clustering matters: the naive iid stat would read "significant," the day-clustered one does not; VAL -0.0112R, day_t flips sign to +0.35). Decomposition: forgone is 67% of fires both reads but carries F5's largest per-fill cost (-0.68R/-0.61R, worse than H25/H26's breakeven-style stops) because a 10-min trailing low is tight enough to get clipped by an ordinary pullback candle -- sum_forgone (-513R/-612R) swamps sum_saved (+436R/+576R) both reads, the widest gap in F5. Consistency: green-week falls hardest of any F5 cell (-14.8pp TRAIN, -13.6pp VAL) yet weekly P10 improves the MOST in F5 (+1.75R TRAIN, +6.21R VAL) and maxdd improves most on VAL (+42R) -- sharpe worsens most of the family (-0.27 TRAIN). WHY: a 10-minute rolling low is the tightest trail in F5 and behaves like tail insurance (best P10/maxdd gain in the family) at the highest cost to median weeks (worst green-week hit) -- exactly the "smooths the tail, taxes the middle" shape the cadence bar exists to catch, and the iid/day-clustered split shows the headline "-2.08" would have overstated the case.

**H28 — trail to day VWAP once run-up hits +1R**
Same +1R gate and fired population as H25/H27 (48.2%/48.1%, fired base_R avg +0.98 both reads) but the loosest trail in the family. dR is F5's closest to flat (+0.0008R TRAIN iid_t 0.10 day_t 0.06; -0.0043R VAL iid_t -0.64 day_t -0.46), ex5 moderately negative both reads (-0.047/-0.050). Decomposition: forgone is 73-74% of fires (same ratio as H25) but forgone per-fill (-0.15R) is F5's smallest in magnitude -- VWAP is a much looser floor than a breakeven stop or a 10-min low, so it gets tested far less often; sum_saved (+125R/+155R) and sum_forgone (-123R/-168R) nearly cancel TRAIN and tip only mildly negative VAL. Consistency: green-week flat TRAIN / -4.5pp VAL, weekly P10 improves both reads (+1.35R/+0.45R, more modestly than H25-27), maxdd improves both (+11R/+19R), sharpe roughly flat. WHY: trailing to VWAP is loose enough to rarely interfere with a genuinely running trade, so it lands closest to a free tail-insurance policy in F5 -- smaller P10/maxdd gains than H25-27 because it rarely gets tested, but also the smallest green-week/median damage of any stop-tightening cell here, the best risk-adjusted shape in the family even though no read clears significance.

## F6 volume / flow mid-flight

**H29 — exit if last-5-bar mean volume <0.3x the breakout bar AND mtm<+0.5R after 20min**
Fires broad and early on the book's laggards: 45.2% (TRAIN-H2, n=2349) / 46.4% (VAL, n=3157) -- the <+0.5R-at-20min gate means fired trades are already skewed negative (base_R mean -0.06/-0.06, median -0.83/-0.85; 61%/60% of fires carry negative base_R). dR is -0.0275R TRAIN (iid_t -1.73, day_t -1.01) / -0.0118R VAL (iid_t -0.87, day_t +0.28, sign flips under clustering) -- neither read clears a bar; ex5 -0.100/-0.087 sits close to raw dR, so this is a broad effect, not a thin-tail artifact. Decomposition: saved wins by count (642/1062=60.5% TRAIN, 875/1464=59.8% VAL, mean +0.75R/+0.78R, sum +482R/+680R) but forgone's per-fill size is ~1.7x larger (-1.30R/-1.22R on 39.5%/40.2% of fires, sum -547R/-717R) -- reconciled net -65R/-37R both reads. Consistency: green-week -18.5pp TRAIN / -4.5pp VAL, weekly P10 +7.23R TRAIN but -1.15R VAL, weekly Sharpe worsens hard both reads (-0.18/-0.08), maxdd improves both (+8.3R/+30.9R). WHY: "volume dried up, still under +0.5R at 20min" correctly flags stalled trades, but ~40% of those stalls are pauses before a real continuation, and that minority's size (forgone -1.2/-1.3R, F6's largest alongside H31) outweighs the majority's smaller saves -- it trims some real losers (maxdd improves) at a heavy cost to green weeks and weekly Sharpe.

**H30 — exit on a red bar with volume >=3x the trailing mean while already >=+0.5R**
F6's narrowest reach: fires 16.7% (TRAIN, n=392) / 18.8% (VAL, n=594) -- requires a red bar AND a 3x volume spike simultaneously while already profitable, so fired trades are already working (base_R mean +0.84/+0.91, median +1.09/+1.13). dR flips sign between reads: +0.0103R TRAIN (iid_t 1.09, day_t -0.02 -- the positive iid read does not survive day-clustering) vs -0.0053R VAL (iid_t -0.67, day_t -0.49); ex5 is -0.070/-0.080 both reads, more negative than raw TRAIN dR, so the one positive point estimate is tail-propped, not broad. Decomposition: saved 43.4%/39.2% of fires (mean +1.13R/+1.02R, sum +192R/+238R) vs forgone 56.6%/60.8% (mean -0.75R/-0.71R, sum -168R/-255R) -- nets positive TRAIN (+24R), negative VAL (-17R), matching the dR flip. Consistency: green-week flat TRAIN / -4.5pp VAL, weekly P10 +3.43R TRAIN but -6.10R VAL (F6's only such reversal), maxdd improves both (+38.7R/+5.9R). WHY: a genuine volume-climax red bar is a real but rare exhaustion tell (<19% reach), and roughly 3-in-5 times it fires on a trade that was only pausing before a bigger continuation (fired median already >+1R) -- the VAL sign flip plus the tail-propped ex5 means this isn't a stable mechanism as specified, just a real but rare signal diluted by a loose volume-spike-only trigger.

**H31 — hold to +1R only while trailing-10-bar up-volume exceeds down-volume, else exit >=+0.5R**
Broad reach, same order as H29: fires 44.5% (TRAIN, n=1045) / 44.7% (VAL, n=1412) once trailing down-volume outweighs up-volume while >=+0.5R; fired trades are already real winners under the base rule (base_R mean +0.72/+0.74, median +1.15/+1.11). dR is negative both reads by iid (-0.0136R t -0.78 TRAIN, -0.0153R t -1.04 VAL) but day-clustered t is weak/sign-inconsistent (-0.46/+0.25); ex5 -0.118/-0.119 is MORE negative than raw dR both reads -- the rule's best outcomes concentrate in a thin right tail. Decomposition: forgone wins on sum both reads despite losing on count -- saved is 42.1%/42.6% of fires but has F6's largest per-fill average (+1.32R/+1.27R, sum +579R/+760R), forgone is 57.9%/57.4% at -1.01R/-1.00R (sum -611R/-809R), net -32R/-48R. Consistency: green-week -11.1pp TRAIN / -18.2pp VAL (F6's worst hit), weekly P10 improves BOTH reads (+9.90R/+4.78R, F6's only such pair and its best), weekly Sharpe worsens most in F6 (-0.17/-0.19), maxdd improves most in F6 (+52.6R/+44.1R). WHY: gating the hold on trailing order flow cuts off 4-in-10 already-working trades at a shallow +0.5R, and because that population is the book's real runners the forgone continuations are as large as H29's (~-1.0R) -- it buys F6's best tail insurance (P10 and maxdd improve on every read) at the worst median cost, the clearest "smooths the tail, taxes the middle" shape in this family, mirroring H27 in F5.

## F7 combinations (pre-declared "secret sauce" candidates)

**H32 — exit when H13 (P(+1R next15)<0.3, >=+0.5R) AND H7 (close<trailing-10m-low, >=+0.5R) both hold**
ANDing two already-narrow F2/F3 triggers collapses the fired population to almost nothing: 0.60% TRAIN (14/2349) / 0.82% VAL (26/3157), versus 16-17% for H13 alone and 22-25% for H7 alone. dR sign-flips across reads (TRAIN -0.0023R, iid_t -1.48, day_t -1.38; VAL +0.0024R, iid_t 1.17, day_t 0.48) and neither clears significance; ex5 stays close to raw dR both reads (-0.0040/-0.0036), so the flip is not a tail artifact, it is just noise on 14-26 trades (MDE 0.0044-0.0058R, the same order as the effect). Decomposition: TRAIN is 5 saved (mean +0.68R) / 9 forgone (mean -0.99R, 64% of fires); VAL is 15 saved (mean +1.22R, 58% of fires) / 11 forgone (mean -0.97R) -- the bucket split itself flips direction between reads. Consistency lens is silent on TRAIN (too few fires to move a week: green-week and P10 both ~unchanged) and mildly positive on VAL (green-week +4.5pp, P10 +0.13R, maxdd +5.3R) -- but that is one week's worth of trades, not a pattern. WHY: requiring a model-probability threshold AND a candle-shape break to agree at the same bar narrows to a population this harness cannot read either way -- ANDing two weak-to-negative singles produces an underpowered cell, not a stronger one.

**H33 — 50% out at +1R (H18), then H24's model-gated +3R target on the remainder**
Fires on the same ~45% of the book that reaches +1R (1068/2349 TRAIN, 1434/3157 VAL) -- H24's P(+1Rnext15)>0.7 gate rarely changes whether "the remainder exists," so this reads almost entirely as H18's variance cut with H24 layered on top. dR is ~flat both reads (TRAIN +0.0002R iid_t 0.03 day_t 0.08; VAL -0.0036R iid_t -0.50 day_t 0.62 -- another iid/day-clustered split) and ex5 is clearly more negative than raw dR both reads (-0.063/-0.064), confirming the near-zero point estimate is a saved/forgone cancellation, not a real edge. Decomposition: saved is ~34% of fires by count both reads (mean +0.79R TRAIN / +0.73R VAL) against 66% forgone (mean -0.41R / -0.40R) -- the same shape as H18 alone. Consistency: green-week flat TRAIN, -4.5pp VAL (H18's known VAL hit reappears unchanged); weekly P10 improves sharply both reads (+5.03R TRAIN, +3.56R VAL) and maxdd improves the most of any F7 cell (+39.9R TRAIN, +29.2R VAL) -- H18's variance-cut signature dominates the combination. WHY: layering H24 on H18's remainder does not change the joint's behavior because H24 alone only fires on 17-19% of +1R-touching trades -- the combination inherits H18's free tail-insurance shape and its VAL green-week cost, with no incremental R edge from the added model gate.

**H34 — exit when H4 (>20min no new high AND mtm<+0.5R) AND H14 (P(stop after k)>0.7, >=+0.25R) both hold**
The narrowest F7 cell: 0.25-0.38% fired (8-9 trades per read) from ANDing two triggers that were themselves negative-leaning in isolation (H4 -0.024R/-0.005R, H14 -0.013R/-0.020R). Reads disagree sharply: TRAIN -0.0041R (iid_t -2.31, day_t -1.79, the family's largest-magnitude clustered t but on n=9) vs VAL +0.0016R (iid_t 1.48, day_t 1.62); ex5 stays near raw dR on VAL (-0.0007, not tail-driven) but the TRAIN/VAL sign flip on 8-9 trades is well inside this cell's MDE (0.003-0.005R). Decomposition: TRAIN is 1 saved (+0.92R) vs 8 forgone (mean -1.32R, 89% of fires); VAL flips to 6 saved (mean +1.17R, 75% of fires) vs 2 forgone (mean -1.01R) -- opposite-shaped buckets, not a stable mechanism. Consistency: TRAIN green-week flat but P10/maxdd both WORSEN slightly (-0.37R / -1.66R, the only F7 cell to worsen maxdd on either read); VAL green-week flat, P10/maxdd improve marginally (+1.21R/+3.72R). WHY: combining two weak stall/exhaustion signals that individually lean negative does not cancel their downsides or compound an edge -- it produces a coin-flip-sized population where the sign is set by which 8-9 trades happen to fire, the same failure mode H15 (H13 AND H14) already showed in F3.

**H35 — H21's breakeven-stop runner AND H8's 5m-close-below-VWAP, combined**
ANDing H21's broad post-+1R gate (45% touch) with H8's near-never VWAP-close trigger (0.1-0.8% alone) collapses to almost nothing: 0.13% TRAIN (3 fills) / 0.03% VAL (1 fill) -- H8's rarity, not H21's breadth, sets the combination's reach. Both reads show a positive point estimate with day-clustered t above 1.5 (TRAIN +0.0063R t_day 1.86; VAL +0.0043R t_day 1.57) and ex5 near zero (-0.0021/-0.0009, not tail-propped on paper) -- but this is 3-4 total trades across the whole book; the "significant" t-stats come from a small, lucky sample's tiny standard error, not a readable signal (MDE 0.0057-0.0087R, the same order as the effect). Decomposition: TRAIN is 2 saved (+0.26R mean) / 1 forgone (-0.53R); VAL is 0 saved / 1 forgone (-0.26R) -- too few fills to have a "split" in any meaningful sense. Consistency lens is essentially silent both reads (green-week 0pp, P10 ~flat, sharpe/maxdd nudge up on 3-4 trades' worth of R). WHY: intersecting a broad rule with an almost-never one just inherits the almost-never one's sample size -- H15's lesson again: a compound trigger is only as informative as its rarest component, and H8's component here is rarer than H14's was in H15. (Despite this, H35 is the TRAIN-scored top of all 35 under the synthesis rule's ex5/P10 gates -- see RESULT_1681.md; the gates pass 3-4 trades because none of them touch a whole week, not because the mechanism is real.)
