# HOD — the holistic, out-of-the-box list (owner 2026-11-12: "find how to exploit weaknesses/strengths/opportunities to make real money from HOD — no more stupid cells, a game changer")

## The one fact everything below is built on
The HOD-break fires ~50 times a week on our universe and, measured live at its own levels, earns **+0.075 R gross** per
fill and pays **≈ 0.15 R in cost** (entry chase 8 bps ≈ 0.05 R on a 1.5 % stop; stop slip 13–35 bps on 41 % stop-outs
≈ 0.05–0.10 R; spread). 2,400+ cells proved the population carries no per-fill information to filter on. So the money
is NOT in picking better breaks. It is in (a) not paying the cost, (b) being paid the cost, (c) changing the instrument
so the cost does not exist, (d) using the signal as information rather than as a trade, and (e) the one strong number
nobody has traded: **a break that fails to fill falls −0.57 R**. Frequency is already there — 50/wk × 0.1 R × $300 R =
$1.5K/wk — the per-fill sign is the whole game.

## A. Be paid the cost instead of paying it (liquidity provision)
1. **Coil bid.** Rest a bid 0.3–0.6 % under the HOD inside the pre-break consolidation (≥ 10 min tight range, ≥ 2 prior
   tests of the high); sell INTO the break at level + 0.3 %. We currently pay the chase and the first leg of the break;
   this collects it. Measure the conditional on fills (dip limits can select broken setups — known risk, measure it).
2. **Retest bid.** After the first 1-min close above HOD, rest a bid AT the level (old resistance = support), stop 0.5 %
   under. Zero chase. The 8,973-row retest dataset (1,478/1,489) already exists — it was built for exactly this and the
   L3 model (AUC 0.72) was withdrawn on a bar-timing bug, never re-run. Finish it.
3. **Bid-side chase.** Enter at the bid, not the ask, for 60 s after the break; accept the lower fill rate. Being
   unfilled is protective (unfilled breaks −0.57 R), so the conditional on our fills improves twice.
4. **Exit into strength.** Half the position as a resting +1.5 R sell limit — the chasers pay the spread to us.
5. **Second mouse.** Skip the first break; enter on the second break after one failure (trapped shorts + fresh longs).
6. **Make the ask wall.** Hold coil inventory and offer at the level: the thin ask that gets eaten is us. (1 + 4 as one
   round trip, measured as a market-making book: fills, inventory, adverse selection.)

## B. Trade the FAILED break — the strongest number in the whole programme
7. **Short the failed break** on easy-to-borrow names: price touches HOD, closes back under within 2 min, no fill. We
   measured −0.57 R from the level. Mechanism: trapped breakout buyers + stop cascade. NOT the "LOD mirror" that failed
   on 9/26 — nobody has run this. ETB list from Alpaca, HTB excluded, cover at VWAP or 15:55.
8. **Put purchase** on the failed break for optionable names (no borrow, defined risk, same mechanism).
9. **Failure-rate tripwire.** When the universe-wide failed-break rate spikes intraday, flatten/pause every long book.
10. **Failed break → VWAP reclaim long.** Buy the flush back through VWAP (a second, cheaper entry into the same name).
11. **Pair the two books.** Long breaks + short failures in the same universe = a market-neutral HOD book; judge the sum.

14. **Short puts into the break** — long exposure with a premium cushion instead of a slipping stop.

18. **Pre-market-high break at the open** (extended-hours level, the first regular-hours break through it).

## F. Structural exit/sizing changes (new mechanisms, not knob cells)
