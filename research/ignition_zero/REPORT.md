# Ignition from zero — REPORT (2026-09-13)

Owner ask (9/13): "I'm sure ignition has an edge. Go back to zero, 15–20 hypotheses on
signals, type of stocks, similar stocks, news, whatever. Find a real valid strategy based
on the principle of ignition; can be different. No bias, no look-ahead, no god mode.
Verify, scrutinize."

Design, splits, hypotheses and verdict rule were pre-registered in `DESIGN.md` before any
number was computed. Nothing below was tuned after seeing TEST. Every table is in
`score_tables.md` (auto-generated by `score.py`); this file is the reading.

## Verdict

**No pre-registered hypothesis survives.** Of 22 hypotheses (H0–H22, H3 with its own entry
simulation), zero produce a cell that is positive ex-tail in TRAIN and VALIDATE and positive
in TEST with at least half its weeks green. The one cell the scorer flagged as a FINDING
(H12 "leader") is a look-ahead artifact and is rejected (section 3).

The ignition principle — a +X% cross from the open, structural stop, held for continuation —
has **no exploitable per-trade edge in a point-in-time universe of 304,799 candidates**
(151,423 symbol-days, 2025-01-02 → 2026-09-11, delisted names included). The base book
(level 10, gated, 9:35–10:30, partial 50%@+1R/BE) is negative ex-tail in every split:

| split | n | meanR ex-tail | meanR | WR | weeks green | worst week (R) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TRAIN 2025 | 13,366 | −0.120 | −0.069 | 42.8% | 16/53 | −396 |
| VAL Jan–May 26 | 6,371 | −0.034 | +0.017 | 49.0% | 12/22 | −38 |
| TEST Jun–Sep 26 | 4,952 | −0.098 | −0.076 | 44.9% | 4/14 | −108 |

This is the same conclusion the live book, the capsim, and the exit study reached from
three other directions. It is now established on the full point-in-time universe, so the
"BT was survivorship-biased / frozen / wrong window" explanations are closed.

## 1. What was built (all point-in-time)

- Universe from Databento EQUS daily bars incl. delisted: open ≥ $1, high/open ≥ 1.05 →
  337,841 symbol-days; 1-min bars from cache.db + top-up + a 19.7M-bar Databento side DB
  (140,509 missing symbol-days fetched, ~$50). Missing bars: 10 rows only.
- Candidates: first cross of +5/+7/+10/+15% from the RTH open on a 1-min bar, 9:35–11:30;
  entry next bar open × 1.003; stop = min(prior-30-min low, entry × 0.99); the three live
  gates (chase, pre-bars, R-min) as FLAGS, not filters; five exits (v0 lock, hold, partial
  50%@+1R/BE, structure trail, time-60). Builder verified bar-exact against the capsim on
  2026-08-05 (31/31 trades).
- Pass 2 features: anchor/wrapper (orb_asset_class), cohort at trigger (causal) and whole
  day (look-ahead, kept for parity only), sympathy lag, market-wide theme heat, news
  (33,118 covered rows; headline class by keyword lists; recency to the trigger minute),
  FINRA short interest as-of `usable_from` (297,060 rows matched), float/sector snapshot.
- Splits fixed: TRAIN 2025 / VAL 2026-01→05 / TEST 2026-06-01→09-11. Verdict rule: TRAIN
  and VAL effect signs agree (|Δ| ≥ 0.02 vs base) AND TEST meanR ex-tail ≥ +0.05 with ≥ half
  weeks green and n ≥ 30.

## 3. The look-ahead the scorer flagged, and why it is rejected

`H12 leader` = "has an anchor group, no sibling triggered before me, and a sibling
triggered LATER today" (built on `coh_day`, the whole-day cohort). The "later" part is
unknowable at the trigger minute. Split the causal set (anchor-bearing, no sibling yet):

| cell | TRAIN | VAL | TEST |
|---|---|---|---|
| anchor & first, all (CAUSAL) | −0.125 | −0.044 | −0.110 |
| … & a sibling follows later (LOOK-AHEAD) | +0.486 | +0.483 | +0.149 |
| … & no sibling follows | −0.135 | −0.076 | −0.131 |

The causal cell equals the base book. The whole effect is "the move continued far enough
that a sibling also crossed" — i.e. the outcome. In a stock+2× wrapper pair the wrapper
crosses +10% first; the stock follows only if the wrapper reaches ~+20%. This is exactly the
god-mode trap the owner named; it is the same mechanism that made the earlier
"complex-confirmed" cell look good when scored with the day-level cohort. **The cohort
feature is only honest as `coh_by_t` (H11), and H11 fails TEST.**

## 4. What this means

1. The edge the owner is sure of is not in the trigger. On 304,799 point-in-time
   candidates, no feature of the stock, the news, the siblings, the market, the short
   interest, the time, the level, the entry, or the exit makes the average ignition trade
   positive out of sample. The positive books ever shown for ignition came from (a) the
   3% tail, (b) survivorship, or (c) look-ahead in the cohort definition.
2. The only in-sample positive structure (siblings/wrappers moving together) is real in
   2025 and Jan–May 2026 and flat in Jun–Sep 2026. It is regime-dependent and there is no
   in-advance signal for the regime in this feature set (H17 SPY context: nothing).
3. Ignition stays PAUSED (dry_run + shadow, since the 9/14 boot). There is no relaunch
   candidate. The 8-straight-positive-weeks roll-forward criterion stands as the only
   reopen path, and the from-zero result says not to expect it.
4. The capital and attention go to the two books with an honest out-of-sample reference:
   ORB B+ (honest $6.6K/21mo at stage size, weekly selection refit) and BF P1 (live from
   9/8 at L0). Both are relative tools, not forecasts; live data decides.

## 5. Things I would still not trust in this study (stated, not hidden)

- News coverage is BT-trade symbol-days only (33K of 305K rows). H14/H15 are read on the
  covered subset; a wider backfill could move them, but both are flat where covered.
- Float/sector are a snapshot, not point-in-time (H7 flat anyway).
- SPY 5-min feature has zero TEST coverage (the cache holds SPY 1-min bars only through
  May 2026). H17 3-day-range is covered and flat.
- The exits are bar-level simulations with 0.1% exit slip and 0.3% entry slip; the live
  fills in Aug–Sep were worse than that (the "rule-violation" losses). A real book would be
  below these numbers, not above.
- 22 hypotheses × 3–5 cells at the 0.05 bar: 3–5 false positives expected by chance. We got
  one, and it was explainable. That is consistent with no signal.
