| **F10** | **The quarantined footprint field, with the gate re-specified** | `dollar_frac` — cumulative $ volume 09:30→signal as a share of 20-day ADV$ — is the ONLY field in 896 cells whose top rung separates gross with the **same sign on both splits** (+0.076 / +0.071), and pass 2's own pre-committed gate excluded it before scoring. **The gate as written is over-broad and that is the finding**: being computed from post-signal data is what makes a field downstream; *marking days that are already wide* is observable at the signal and is a statement about WHICH HALF of the cohort a field selects, not a causality violation. F10 re-specifies the already-there / arrived-after split as a **reported diagnostic**, not an exclusion, and pre-registers: decile ladder on `dollar_frac`, the era-consistency requirement (same sign in H1, H2 and VAL) as the ONLY selector, the cross with the shipped stack, and a dedicated NBBO fetch at the selected rung's own minutes (its cost is 20-40 % imputed). Mechanism: half a day's average dollar volume done before 10:30 is size participating, not the tape churning — the one thing in this programme that is about WHO is buying rather than about the shape of the bars. |
| **F11** | **Identify `consol_bars`, the last unexplained survivor** | `consol_bars >= 20` (`hod_fresh` C1) is the only admission in 896 cells same-signed positive in H1, H2 and VAL, and F6 has now refuted both natural mechanism stories for it — shelf volume at the level and the age of the high are each NEGATIVE. An admission whose mechanism is unknown cannot be trusted at 13.8/wk and cannot be extended or ported to another book. The frame decomposes the field into its candidate contents and scores each SEPARATELY on the same cascade: (a) the rising-lows sequence (higher lows into the break, which is what "every low within 4 % of the level for 20 bars" mostly is), (b) range compression into the break (ATR14 falling, the classic coil), (c) the entry minute it implies (20 bars of holding cannot happen before 09:56 — the clock confound that killed `add30_ratio`), (d) the price band and the spread it selects. If (c) or (d) carries the sign, C1 is a clock or a liquidity cell and the programme's one survivor is retired; if (a) or (b) does, there is a mechanism to build on. |
| **F12** | **ADAPTIVE vs FROZEN — refit the admission on a rolling window** (absorbs F7) | Every strong separation this programme has found changes sign between 2025 and 2026: entry minute (+0.518 the strongest single feature, and the direction flips), `exp5_n` (+0.232 → −0.145), `add30_ratio` (t +10.14 → −0.094), `rv >= 5` (+0.177 in `hod_break` → negative in `hod_fresh`), the SPY 09:35 gate (+0.725 H1 → −0.041 H2 on the base). **Nine passes have asked "which frozen rule is right" and the data keeps answering "it depends on the regime".** ORB settled the identical question on 2026-09-08 with the honest dump: refitting the SELECTION weekly on a rolling 26-week window beats frozen ($7,588 vs $5,669, MDD −223 vs −551, robust for windows ≥ 20 weeks) while refitting the SIZING is the whipsaw. HOD-break has **never** been tested with a rolling refit of anything. The frame: walk forward week by week, refit ONLY the admission window (the hour band, plus one ranked feature cut) on the trailing 26 weeks, book the next week out of sample, and compare against the frozen book on the owner's metric — with the pre-committed rails that the refit may never see the week it trades, that the window length is declared in advance (26 weeks, with 20 and 34 as robustness arms), and that a refit book must beat frozen on BOTH TRAIN halves and VAL to count. This also subsumes the queued F7 (entry-minute × SPY-state), which is one frozen cell of the same map. |
