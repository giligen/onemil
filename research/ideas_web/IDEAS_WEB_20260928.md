## Considered and discarded (thin mechanism, own-literature negative, or out of scope)

- **Retail order-flow imbalance as a return predictor** — the academic literature this scan found explicitly
  says predictability exists but is "not profitable after trading costs" for the retail side itself (a paradox:
  retail is the buyer at the high it creates). Discard.
- **Short-interest/borrow-rate squeeze screening** — the sources' own framing calls it "high-risk speculation,
  not a reliable strategy"; no mechanism beyond crowd-chasing a catalyst. Discard per the task's own
  "discretionary content" criterion.
- **NextVoL-style daily vol-targeting overlay** (scale exposure to hold realized vol constant, Sharpe 0.79 SPY
  2008-2025) — not a new alpha source, a position-sizing overlay. Worth a note for the owner as a possible
  cross-book sizing research question, not a new "book."
- **Low-risk factor, calendar seasonality, FX hedging** (Quantpedia top-10 items) — real but monthly-cadence,
  crowded, or (FX) out of scope for a US-equities day-trading engine; kept in the ranked table (6, 10) rather
  than discarded outright since the mechanism is real, but ranked low on feasibility/frequency fit.
- **Futures-based ideas (Russell reconstitution via RTY futures, CTA trend-following)** — mechanism is real but
  the retail-scale implementation path assumed by every source is futures, and **we have no futures account**;
  the equity-side version (idea #4) is what's listed instead.

## Recommended test order

1. **Overnight index/ETF volume-shape drift (#2)** — cheapest to falsify (EQUS + Databento intraday volume,
   no new data source), stack-native via Alpaca's 24/5 session, and mechanistically distinct enough from our
   closed overnight-hold work to be worth a from-zero PIT study.
2. **Earnings IV-crush selectivity (#1)** — clear quantified filter (realized/implied ratio) to reimplement and
   check causally; options L3 + Databento OPRA already support it; defined-risk by construction.
3. **Dealer GEX regime filter (#3)** — highest optionality (0DTE SPX/SPY is liquid, matches "ODTE" ask directly)
   but needs an in-house GEX definition built and PIT-tested before it's a claim, since every source is a vendor
   selling the same number differently.

Both #4 (index reconstitution) and #5 (pairs) are worth a lightweight feasibility check (data source for #4,
Alpaca shortability screen for #5) before committing research time, given their capacity/frequency and
borrow-risk caveats respectively.
